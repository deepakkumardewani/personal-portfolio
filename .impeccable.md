## Design Context

### Users

Three audiences, one site:

1. **Recruiters / hiring managers** — evaluating Deepak for senior/staff frontend roles; need to feel seniority within 3 seconds; will share URL with their team if impressed
2. **Freelance clients** — startup founders or PMs looking for a senior contractor; need to see delivery track record and judgment, not just a skill list
3. **Developer peers** — fellow frontend engineers who encounter the site via a share; the most critical audience technically; they will notice lazy choices

Context: visited on desktop and mobile, likely during a work day, often in a tab alongside other portfolios. Must stand out immediately.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **precise, authoritative, unhurried**

- Precise: every pixel, every timing value, every spacing choice is deliberate — nothing is default
- Authoritative: 8 years, one company, four promotions — this is someone who has earned his position; the site doesn't beg for attention
- Unhurried: confident pacing in animations; nothing rushes or bounces; the work speaks for itself

Emotional goal: visitors should feel they are looking at the work of a craftsperson — not a resume page, not an AI-generated template.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Dark, typographic, editorial-industrial.**

- Near-black background (`#080808`) — not pure black, not charcoal gray
- Electric indigo accent (`#6366f1`) — used sparingly; CTAs and highlights only
- Off-white text (`#f0ede8`) — never harsh white
- Typography carries the visual weight — no hero images, no decorative illustrations
- GSAP scroll animations are the primary visual spectacle; everything else is restrained
- The site feels like a high-end engineering portfolio crossed with an editorial magazine spread

**Fonts (fixed — do not change without asking):**

- Display / Hero: `Bebas Neue` — condensed, tall, typographic authority
- Body / labels: `DM Mono` — monospaced precision signals engineering background
- UI: `Instrument Sans` — clean, contemporary (user's explicit choice; retained despite being a common font)

**Anti-references:** generic Tailwind portfolios, Notion-style minimal white pages, floating cards with drop shadows, gradient text, any layout that looks like it came from a template.

### Design Principles

1. **The site is the proof** — every visual and interaction decision must demonstrate frontend craft, not describe it
2. **Restraint creates impact** — the accent color works because it's rare; animations work because they're purposeful, not decorative
3. **Typography first** — Bebas Neue at scale IS the hero; never add imagery where type can do the job
4. **Scroll is the narrative** — the page has a story arc: hero → work → growth → recognition → skills → contact; don't break the flow
5. **No AI tells** — no gradient text, no side-stripe card borders, no glassmorphism, no bouncy easing, no sparklines, no identical card grids
